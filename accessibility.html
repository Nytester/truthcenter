Accessibility Statement

Last updated: August 30, 2026

This is a general accessibility statement template. It reflects an intended commitment and should be updated to describe your actual accessibility testing and remediation efforts.

Our Commitment

The Truth Center is committed to ensuring digital accessibility for people of all abilities. We are continually working to improve the accessibility of our website for everyone, including individuals with disabilities.

Conformance Goal

We aim for our website to conform to the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1, Level AA, which help make web content more accessible to people with a wide range of disabilities.
